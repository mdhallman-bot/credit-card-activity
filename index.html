Rewards Lab

Your mission: Figure out when rewards actually help—and when the costs wipe them out. Start with the example shown. Change one variable at a time and watch the result.
Investigate:
1. Increase eligible spending. What happens to rewards?
2. Add a $95 annual fee. What happens to the net benefit?
3. Add $220 of interest. Are the rewards still helping?
4. Complete the Trap Test.
Reasoning frame: The rewards seem / do not seem worthwhile because _____ in rewards is compared with _____ in costs.
7

Balance-Transfer Lab

Your mission: Decide whether moving debt to a promotional offer actually helps this borrower. A balance transfer moves debt. It does not erase it.
Investigate:
1. Notice the transfer fee and the new starting balance.
2. See whether $300/month clears the debt during 12 promotional months.
3. Change the offer to 6 months, 5% fee, $150/month.
4. Decide what becomes risky when the promotional period ends.
Before accepting a 0% offer, check all four: fees, how long the promotional rate lasts, the APR after the promotion, and what happens if a payment is late.
Reasoning frame: This offer could help _____ if _____; however, _____.
8

Choose Your Move — Boss Battle

Three people. Three problems. Choose a move and defend it.

A — Rewards Trap

Aaliyah earned $180 in rewards but paid $420 in interest. She says, “They literally pay me to use this card.”
Sentence frame: The rewards did not make up for _____ because _____. Aaliyah should _____.

B — The 0% Offer

Marcus owes $3,000 at 25% APR. Offer: 0% for 12 months + 3% fee. He can pay $300/month and will stop new charges.
Sentence frame: This transfer can / cannot make sense because _____. The most important condition is _____.

C — I Can't Pay

Elena owes $2,400. Minimum: $85. Her hours were cut and she can afford $50 this month. If she simply misses payments, her card agreement may allow late fees, higher costs or rates, and other consequences.
Sentence frame: My work hours were reduced and I cannot make the full minimum payment. I can afford _____ right now, and I would like to ask about _____.

Final Mission — Find Your Card

Research a real credit card that could fit your life now or in the near future. You are evaluating publicly available terms—not applying for credit.

⚠ RESEARCH ONLY — DO NOT APPLY

DO NOT ENTER YOUR NAME, ADDRESS, PHONE NUMBER, EMAIL, DATE OF BIRTH, INCOME, SOCIAL SECURITY NUMBER (SSN), BANK INFORMATION, OR ANY OTHER PERSONAL INFORMATION.

DO NOT CLICK “APPLY,” “APPLY NOW,” “PREQUALIFY,” “CHECK MY OFFERS,” OR ANY BUTTON THAT ASKS FOR PERSONAL INFORMATION.

You should be able to complete this entire mission without telling a website anything about yourself. If a site asks for personal information, STOP and go back.
